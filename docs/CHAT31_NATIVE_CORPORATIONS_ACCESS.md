# Chat 31 - native player corporations: persistent-role and asset-custody boundary

**Status: CODE_READY / LIVE_UNVERIFIED; no production deployment.** Pinned original `gameboys84/EveJS@c3ea396b6761a0ed3e71ad5b8dd7507f9ae6d69f`. Repo integration is **source-level proof**, not a second corporation, inventory, or wallet implementation. NPC corporations, NPC economic settlement and fleet membership are deliberately outside this patch.

## Existing native capabilities (preserved, not reimplemented)

The pinned upstream contains real `corpRegistryRuntime` creation, applications, invitations and member records; `corporationRuntimeState` roles, division metadata and corporation wallets; `officeManagerService` office rental and impound; `corpWalletState` transaction ledgers; `corpAssetState` asset views; `invBrokerService` native inventory transfer and `industryRuntimeState` corporate jobs and wallet quotes. This commit does **not** claim these routes are end-to-end verified with two actual authenticated clients. Native corporations already use persisted corporation IDs, not fleet IDs.

## Source-level defects and specific opt-in repair

1. Native `industryAccess.js` accepts cached session roles and treats selecting `corpAccountKey` as permission to spend that wallet division. `canTakeFromOwnerLocation` also lets Factory Manager take any corporation hangar division without an explicit TAKE role. This can authorize an outdated or overprivileged client after role revocation.
2. `invBrokerService.js` checks division TAKE narrowly in `Handle_Add`, but the shared `_moveSourceItemToDestination` receives transfers from `MultiAdd`, fitting and `DeliverToCorpHangar`; `_tryLoadChargeFromInventoryAdd` runs before that shared transfer. Those paths must not provide a different item-custody permission rule. Stock corporate hangar listing may also leak when role information is stale.
3. `corpRegistryRuntime.js` can process several native invitations, applications, and member-role edits without checking the actual caller at the mutation boundary. In particular, applicant acceptance must be tied to a **pre-existing accepted invitation/application and applicant identity**, not only a client-supplied `status=2`.
4. `corpMemberActionState.js` processes cash/share grants with separate writes to corporate and character financial stores. There is no native per-RPC durable operation receipt proving replay identity and no single transaction covering both. A retry or crash can lose or duplicate funds; under this new opt-in security guard, unsafe grant actions **fail closed** instead of pretending they are atomic.

**Added `integration/coopPlayerCorporationAccess.cjs`:** read-only authorization using `getCorporationRecord`, `getCorporationMember`, `getCharacterRecord`, `getCorporationRuntime`, `getCorporationOffices`, actual native inventory parent chain and the real docked location. Every call rechecks the authoritative member and corporation IDs. The source of rights is saved member roles, not a role mask, wallet key or fleet designation from the session. CEO identity is validated against the corporation record. A Factory Manager can run industry and view/take industry deliveries but cannot withdraw ordinary hangars or corporate ISK without their respective grants. TAKE and QUERY for all seven hangar divisions are independent; wallet TAKE for divisions 1000-1006 is independent of selected `corpAccountKey`. Corporate hulls outside authorized divisions require a director.

**Added `scripts/coop-player-corporation-access-patch.cjs`:** explicitly reversible, LF/CRLF-agnostic, unique-anchor, default-OFF one-call hooks for pinned `industryAccess.js`, `invBrokerService.js`, `corpRegistryRuntime.js`, `corpMemberActionState.js`, `officeManagerService.js`. The native writers remain the only custody and wallet authorities. Legitimate personal-to-corporation donations require a real active office, matching docked station, and source root at the same station. A foreign owner's station cargo cannot be taken by guessing an `itemID`. Existing native wreck transfer retains its **separate** loot-rights handler; the guard does not turn fleet members into corporate members.

The new environment switch is `EVEJS_COOP_PLAYER_CORP_ACCESS=1` **only on an isolated, disposable test world**, and **OFF by default**. The central installer module copy list and source hook specs are owned by Chat 01; they are intentionally **not changed here**. This candidate is not live-enabled, and is not automatically installed by the current overlay.
